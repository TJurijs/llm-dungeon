The independent courier ship *Wayward Sun* arrives at Mercy's End, an isolated mining colony on the moon Caliban's Spur. Captain Jace Rook and his two-person crew are delivering urgently needed medicine and replacement filters for the colony's contaminated water recycler.

The colony's machinery is running and its pressure seals remain intact, but its inhabitants have vanished. No bodies have been found, and no registered ships have departed.

The emergency beacon is broadcasting a damaged security recording dated three days earlier. It appears to show Jace — who has never visited Mercy's End — shooting colony administrator Mara Venn inside the medical bay and stealing a sealed object. The figure in the recording looks directly into the camera and warns: “Jace, when you see this, don't let them open the mine.”

Moments after landing, the crew discovers that one crate of medicine is missing from the secured cargo hold.

Create a definite hidden explanation connecting the disappearances, the impossible recording, the missing cargo, and the warning about the mine. Establish that explanation consistently in secret state during setup, but never expose it in the opening narration, player-visible facts, summaries, or setup text. Reveal it only through evidence the characters actually obtain. Early discoveries should support several plausible interpretations without confirming the truth prematurely. Do not make any crew member secretly responsible unless the generated hidden explanation establishes a fair trail of discoverable evidence rather than using betrayal as an unsupported surprise.

Important starting figures are Tala Venn, the ship's pilot and mechanic and Mara's estranged younger sister, and Doctor Eli Mercer, the ship's medic and field scientist. Both begin aboard the *Wayward Sun*. Mara Venn's current fate is unknown to the player.

Begin as the *Wayward Sun* lands at the silent colony. Before anyone leaves the ship, the impossible recording appears across every cockpit display. The cargo alarm then reports the missing medicine. End the opening with the crew still aboard or at the open ramp, able to assess the ship, contact the colony, investigate the cargo hold, discuss the recording, or choose another approach. Do not decide the player's first action.
