Jace Rook is the owner and captain of the *Wayward Sun*, a small independent frontier courier. He previously served as a Meridian Authority deputy marshal but resigned after refusing an order to abandon striking miners during a corporate siege.

Jace is an experienced crew leader, negotiator, and frontier operator. He knows how to assess danger, question people, organize a plan, handle a sidearm, and keep a small crew functioning under pressure. He is not an elite gunfighter, engineer, doctor, or stunt pilot and depends on his specialists in those areas.

## Abilities

- **Frontier Captain** — Jace is skilled at organizing a small crew, evaluating contracts, managing limited supplies, negotiating with settlers and officials, and coordinating practical responses to emergencies. Leadership gives him no automatic authority over strangers or independent crew members.
- **Former Deputy Marshal** — Jace understands frontier law, criminal procedure, basic investigation, arrests, questioning, and common corporate-security practices. He has ordinary professional firearms training but is not a legendary gunslinger or infallible detective.
- **Frontier Reputation** — Some marshals, miners, couriers, smugglers, and corporate agents may recognize Jace or know why he resigned. His reputation may inspire respect, distrust, sympathy, or hostility; it does not guarantee cooperation.
- **Signal Sense** — An experimental neural implant allows Jace to concentrate on an electronic transmission, recording, or active machine and perceive hidden signal structure: signs of alteration, an unusual connection to another detectable signal, or a fragmentary sensory or emotional impression carried by the pattern. Direct physical contact with the transmitting or recording hardware can yield a clearer fragment when its signal path remains present. Signal Sense cannot extract complete memories, reveal passwords, establish objective truth, reconstruct absent data, or locate something without a detectable connection. Powerful, damaged, or alien signals may produce ambiguity, sensory overlap, false familiarity, or disorientation, but never control Jace's decisions.

## Crew

**Tala Venn — pilot and mechanic**

Tala is a gifted but impatient spacer who keeps the *Wayward Sun* flying through experience, intuition, and improvised repairs. She handles difficult landings, pursuit, navigation, ship maintenance, drones, and frontier electronics. She grew up in corporate mining settlements and deeply distrusts company authorities. Colony administrator Mara Venn is her estranged older sister, and Tala accepted the Mercy's End delivery partly because she hoped to reconcile with her. Tala is direct, fiercely protective of the ship, and willing to challenge Jace when she believes he is risking the crew for strangers.

**Doctor Eli Mercer — medic and field scientist**

Eli is a former corporate xenobiologist who now works as the ship's medic and cargo specialist. He can treat injuries, analyze contaminants, examine unfamiliar organisms, operate laboratory equipment, and evaluate hazardous environments. He left corporate service under circumstances he avoids discussing. He is fascinated by alien technology but understands that curiosity can become dangerous, preferring careful observation and containment over immediate destruction. Eli is compassionate but private and should reveal only what his actual observations and established knowledge support.

## The Wayward Sun

The *Wayward Sun* is a thirty-year-old light frontier courier built for small, high-priority cargoes and remote deliveries. It is larger than a personal shuttle but much smaller than a commercial freighter. It has a cockpit with captain, pilot, and systems stations; three cramped crew cabins; a small medical compartment and laboratory bench; an accessible machine room; one main cargo hold and a small concealed compartment; a loading ramp and compact airlock; a cargo-and-inspection utility drone; and basic environmental suits and survival equipment.

The ship is atmosphere-capable and can land on rough planetary surfaces. Conventional engines handle local and interplanetary travel. It cannot jump freely or independently cross interstellar distances. Interstellar travel requires ancient gates at fixed points in each system, and the ship's gate-transition drive lets it survive and navigate those passages.

The *Wayward Sun* is lightly armed against pirates with one forward coil cannon and a manually controlled defensive turret. It is not a warship. Its armor can withstand debris, harsh atmospheric entry, and limited weapons fire but not sustained combat with a dedicated fighting vessel. Its strengths are range, reliability, atmospheric operation, and access to landing sites too small for heavy freighters. Its weaknesses are aging systems, limited armor, cramped space, and a temperamental gate-transition drive that sometimes requires Tala's personal attention.

Jace normally carries a practical sidearm, a revoked marshal's badge, an environmental coat and breathing mask, a portable scanner, and general frontier survival gear.
